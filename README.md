# Research Collaboration & Lab Matching Platform

### Overview
Research is getting more interdisciplinary, and finding the right collaborator is getting harder. A biology student may need help from someone who knows machine learning. An environmental scientist might need GIS. Nobody knows who to ask, and cold emails mostly go unanswered. On the professor side, research interests sit on outdated pages that nobody updates.

This project fixes that with a UBC-specific platform. Students build a profile of research interests and technical skills, and the platform matches them against live faculty research, publications, and lab pages, recommending people and labs whose work actually aligns. It can also draft a personalized outreach email so the first message isn't the hardest part. If time allows, we aggregate research opportunities: RA openings, awards, directed studies, volunteer positions.

### What you'll work on

- A research knowledge base: crawling and structuring faculty pages, lab sites, publications
- Research profiles for students: interests, skills, experience
- Semantic matching with embeddings, plus recommendations you can explain
- The invitation generator that writes a decent first email
- Opportunity discovery, the stretch goal
